# Research: Fees, Payments, Portals & Software in Nigerian Tertiary Institutions

Compiled 2026-10-01 from web sources (cited inline).

## 1. Fee Types and Structures

### 1.1 The anatomy of a Nigerian institution's fee schedule
Nigerian tertiary fees are **not a single "tuition" number** — they are an itemized bundle of
charges that varies by: **institution type** (federal/state/private), **level** (100L fresh
students always pay more than returning students), **programme/faculty** (science/lab/clinical
programmes pay more), and — at state universities — **state of origin (indigene vs
non-indigene)**. The fee schedule is keyed on at least:
`session × programme/department × level × student_category (fresh/returning/final-year/DE) × indigene_status`.

Typical line items found on fee breakdowns:

| Fee item | Notes / typical range |
|---|---|
| Acceptance fee | One-off, paid on admission before registration. ₦5,000–₦10,000 at many federal schools; state schools differentiate — e.g. DELSU ~₦30,000 (indigene) vs ₦40,000 (non-indigene); KWASU ₦30,000–₦35,000 vs ₦35,000–₦50,000 |
| School fees / tuition + sundry charges | The main per-session bundle (see 1.2) |
| ICT / e-portal / technology fee | ₦5,000–₦10,000 |
| Faculty dues | ₦5,000–₦15,000 |
| Departmental dues | ₦3,000–₦8,000 |
| Library fee | Usually bundled, a few thousand naira |
| Medical/health services fee + medical screening (fresh students) | Bundled or separate |
| Laboratory/studio/fieldwork fee | Only for lab/studio programmes (UNILAG: returning without lab ₦100,750 vs with lab ₦140,250) |
| Hostel/accommodation fee | Separate, optional, per bed space (see 1.3) |
| Utility fee | Common at federal schools post-2023 |
| Late registration penalty | Commonly ₦5,000–₦20,000; configurable surcharge with a cutoff date |
| Matriculation fee / ID card fee | Fresh students |
| Convocation fee | Final-year students only |
| Transcript fee | On-demand service, not sessional (see 1.4) |
| Caution/damages deposit | Fresh students, sometimes refundable |
| GST/entrepreneurship, sports, exam fees | Frequently itemized separately |

### 1.2 Typical total amounts by institution type (per session)
- **Federal universities** (post-2023/24 fee increases): roughly ₦100,000–₦200,000/session.
  UNILAG 2024/25: fresh ₦176,325; returning (no lab) ₦100,750; returning (lab/studio) ₦140,250;
  returning medical ₦190,250. UNIBEN: ₦160,000–₦190,000 by course. BUK fresh: ~₦97,000–₦171,300
  by faculty. Tuition itself is officially "free" at federal universities — the charges are
  sundry/service fees, which is why schedules are so itemized.
  ([Edusko UNILAG](https://www.edusko.com/blog/university-of-lagos-unilag-school-fees-for-the-20242025-academic-session),
  [MyUni federal list](https://myuni.com.ng/list-of-federal-universities-in-nigeria-and-their-school-fees/))
- **State universities**: wider range, with indigene discounts. OOU: ₦77,872–₦173,396 (indigene)
  vs ₦80,872–₦178,000 (non-indigene) by course. EKSU, KWASU, DELSU publish dual-rate tables.
- **Private universities**: ₦800,000–₦6,000,000+/session. Covenant ~₦1.71m–₦2.01m; Babcock
  ~₦813k–₦2.0m; ABUAD ₦1.1m–₦6.17m (Medicine ~₦5.55m).
  ([Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/07/04/top-10-most-expensive-private-universities-in-nigeria-for-2025-2026-2/))
- **Polytechnics**: generally cheaper than universities (ND/HND fee tables per department).
  ([Ibadan Poly](https://myschoolguild.com.ng/ibadan-polytechnic-school-fees-2025-2026-session/))
- **Post-UTME/screening application fee**: ~₦2,000–₦17,000.

### 1.3 Hostel fees
Per-session, per-bed-space, varies by hostel type: FUTA ₦8,090–₦75,090 by hostel; FUD ₦40,000;
FUOTUOKE ₦30,000; FUHSA ₦100,000. FUNAAB runs **competitive online bidding** with a
non-refundable ₦2,500 bidding fee — bed spaces are scarce, so first-come/bid/ballot allocation is
a real feature requirement.
([FUNAAB hostel](https://myschoolgist.com/news/funaab-hostel-accomodation-registration/),
[FUTA fees](https://www.teezab.com/futa-school-fees/))

### 1.4 Transcript fees
Local vs international rates, often with a separate application fee: UNN ₦20,000 local / ₦35,000
international; UNIBEN ₦20,000/₦50,000; UNILAG ₦10,000 application + ₦20,000–₦35,000; UI
₦25,000/₦35,000; ABU ₦5,000 (local e-copy), ₦15,000 (local hard copy), ₦50,000 (international
hard copy) — note e-copy vs hard-copy and destination-based pricing. Public universities earned
~₦12bn from transcripts in 5 years.
([Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/features/amid-hurdles-public-varsities-make-n12b-from-transcript-requests-in-5yrs/),
[ABU transcript portal](https://transcript.abu.edu.ng/))

## 2. Payment Infrastructure

### 2.1 Remita / TSA / RRR (federal institutions — effectively mandatory)
- Since the 2015 full TSA (Treasury Single Account) directive, **federal universities and
  polytechnics must collect all revenue through Remita into the CBN-held TSA**. Remita (by
  SystemSpecs) has been the FGN TSA gateway since 2012.
  ([Wikipedia: Remita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remita),
  [TSA Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasury_single_account_(Nigeria)))
- **Flow**: student logs into portal → portal generates an invoice and calls Remita to generate
  an **RRR (Remita Retrieval Reference)** — a unique reference bound to payer, institution, exact
  amount and purpose → student pays that RRR **online (card/transfer/USSD) or at any bank branch
  over the counter** → Remita notifies the portal (webhook/status query) → portal marks the fee
  paid and **unlocks course registration and other services**.
  ([brands.ng RRR guide](https://brands.ng/blog/remita-rrr-payment/),
  [UNIABUJA Remita procedure](https://schoolgist.ng/uniabuja-fees-payment-procedure-via-remita))
- **Implementation implications**: invoice/RRR lifecycle (generated → pending → paid →
  confirmed/reconciled), re-queryable RRR status (bank-branch payments confirm asynchronously,
  sometimes 24h+), printable invoices and receipts bearing the RRR, split-settlement support.

### 2.2 Other gateways (state/private institutions and secondary charges)
- **Paystack** — most popular modern gateway; discounted rates for schools; cards, transfers,
  USSD, virtual accounts.
- **Flutterwave** — used by school ERPs to give each student a **unique virtual account** so
  any-channel payments auto-match to the student invoice.
- **Interswitch WebPay** — the oldest (2002); 1.5% capped at ₦2,000; long history with university
  portals.
- **Monnify (Moniepoint)** — popular for dedicated virtual accounts per student; also eTranzact,
  Squad (GTCO). Bank-branch payment against a reference remains essential for students without
  cards.
  ([Businessday gateway comparison](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/how-seven-payment-gateways-stack-up-for-nigerian-businesses/))
- **Design takeaway**: gateway-abstraction layer (Remita, Paystack, Flutterwave, Interswitch,
  Monnify drivers) + manual/bank-teller confirmation with reconciliation.

### 2.3 Installment policies
Widespread and institution-specific — split percentages and deadlines must be configurable per
session/level:
- **Bowen University**: 100L/DE/transfer: 70% at session start, 30% at second-semester start;
  200L–500L: 40% / 10% (before 1st-sem exams) / 30% / 20% (before 2nd-sem exams).
  ([Bowen tuition](https://bowen.edu.ng/tuition-fee/))
- **UNILAG** publishes a formal "Procedure for Instalment Payment of Fees".
- Some schools are reverting: **FUTIA abolished its 70/30 policy** — now 100% at registration.
- Common enforcement: first installment before course registration opens; full balance before
  exam card printing / exam clearance; late registration penalty after deadline.

## 3. Student Portal Features (student-facing)

Canonical feature set from live portals (UNN/Tenece, UNIUYO, UNIBEN Kofa, SAFSRMS schools):

1. **Fees & payments**: fee schedule per session; invoice/RRR generation; online payment;
   payment history with printable receipts; payment status gating all other actions.
2. **Course registration**: per-semester registration from the programme's course list;
   **carryover courses registered first**; credit-unit validation (min ~15, max 24/semester,
   24 often allowed only with carryovers); **add/drop window** (first 1–2 weeks of semester,
   adviser/HOD approval); printable **course registration form** (signed copies).
3. **Results**: per-semester results (grades, GPA, CGPA), result slips, carryover flags.
4. **Exam clearance / exam card**: printable exam card generated only when fees fully paid and
   courses registered.
5. **Hostel booking**: availability, booking/bidding, allocation, payment, bed-space slip.
6. **Biodata management**: profile/passport, next-of-kin, O'level & JAMB details, state/LGA
   (drives indigene pricing); locked after matriculation, changes via admin request.
7. **Document printing**: admission letter, acceptance letter, matric slip, course forms, exam
   cards, receipts, clearance slips.
8. **Admission-stage screens**: admission status check, acceptance-fee payment, online clearance
   (O'level upload, JAMB admission letter, birth cert, LGA identification).
9. **Transcript request**: apply online, local/international + destination, pay, track status.
10. Misc: e-learning materials, library access, complaints/ticketing, ID-card request.

## 4. Staff-Side Features (academic workflow)

### 4.1 Result processing pipeline
1. **Lecturer**: enters CA (typically 30–40 marks) + exam (60–70) per registered student; bulk
   spreadsheet upload is a must; mark sheets auto-populate only registered students.
2. **Exam officer (departmental)**: collates, checks completeness, computes GPA/CGPA, flags
   carryovers and probation.
3. **HOD / Departmental Board**: vets and approves.
4. **Faculty Board / Dean**: approves faculty-wide results.
5. **Senate (university) / Academic Board (polytechnic)**: final approval — only then are
   results released to students and usable for transcripts/NYSC lists.
6. Post-approval: publication, amendment workflow with audit trail (amendments also require
   Senate ratification).

System needs: role-based states per result batch (draft → submitted → HOD-approved →
faculty-approved → senate-approved → published), stage locking, audit logs.
([Hybrid result system paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/338745216_Development_of_Hybrid_Result_Processing_and_Management_System_for_Nigerian_Universities),
[Kwara Poly result platform](https://results.kwarastatepolytechnic.edu.ng/))

### 4.2 Transcript processing workflow
Application (online) → payment → records/exams unit pulls Senate-approved results → compilation &
verification → signed by Registrar/Deputy Registrar (Exams & Records) → dispatch (e-copy direct
to destination institution, or courier). Typical SLA 1–3 weeks; status tracking is a key
differentiator because delays are notorious.

### 4.3 Other staff features
- Course allocation per semester; class lists/attendance sheets from registration data.
- Dean/HOD dashboards: registration stats, payment compliance, result status by course.
- Exam management: timetable, venue allocation, invigilator assignment.

## 5. Other Administrative Modules

### 5.1 Admissions management
Candidate applies (post-UTME/screening) → screening on JAMB score + O'level (verified on **JAMB
CAPS** — Central Admissions Processing System) → composite score → **admission lists in batches:
Merit, 2nd/3rd batch, supplementary** → upload recommended candidates to CAPS → candidate accepts
on CAPS → acceptance fee on institution portal → online clearance → matriculation number
generation. Track CAPS status fields, cut-off marks per programme, quota (merit ~45%, catchment,
ELDS).
([JAMB CAPS guide](https://www.tutorialhaven.com.ng/blog/jamb-caps-your-essential-guide-to-securing-university-admission-in-nigeria/))

### 5.2 Student ID cards
Generated after clearance/matriculation: biodata + passport + matric number + barcode/QR; reprint
flow with police report/affidavit + fee.

### 5.3 Clearance workflows
- **Entry clearance** (fresh students): document verification (O'level, JAMB letter, birth
  certificate, LGA/indigene certificate, medical).
- **Final-year clearance**: multi-unit sign-off — department, faculty, library, bursary, hostel,
  sports, alumni; outcome feeds statement of result and NYSC lists.

### 5.4 NYSC mobilization (universities & HND)
Institutions submit the **Senate/Academic Board-approved graduate list** to NYSC per mobilization
batch (A/B/C). New since Oct 2025: **NERD clearance** — graduates upload final-year project to the
national NERD database for a National Credential Number before camp registration. A "generate
NYSC mobilization list (Senate-approved, by batch)" export is a concrete feature.
([NERD for NYSC](https://myschoolgist.com/news/upload-final-year-thesis-to-nerd-for-nysc/))

### 5.5 SIWES / IT placement (polytechnics + science/tech programmes)
ITF-run scheme, minimum 24 weeks for ND students. System needs: SIWES registration, placement
records (company, supervisor), ITF forms (SPE-1, Form 8, bio-data), introduction/acceptance
letters, weekly logbook tracking, supervision visit records (≥2), report grading, ITF allowance
bank details.
([ITF SIWES](https://itf.gov.ng/pdf/SIWES%20Work.pdf))

### 5.6 Staff/HR basics
- **University academic ranks (CONUASS)**: Graduate Assistant → Assistant Lecturer → Lecturer II
  → Lecturer I → Senior Lecturer → Reader/Associate Professor → Professor.
- **Polytechnic ranks (CONPCASS)**: Assistant Lecturer/Lecturer III → Lecturer II → Lecturer I →
  Senior Lecturer → Principal Lecturer → Chief Lecturer.
- Non-academic cadres on CONTISS/CONTEDISS. Designations drive workflow roles (HOD, Dean, exam
  officer, course adviser).
